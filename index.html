about · mars

MARS takes an open-weights model and sends it on one mission: your workflow. Not a benchmark, not a demo set. The actual tasks your team runs.

The loop is short. Observe real traces. Evaluate every output against evals you write as code. Refine by fine-tuning on what passed. Ascend only when the new weights beat the old ones. Then go again.

Day 100 beats day 1. The weights stay yours, small enough to run on hardware you own.

M·A·S for mastery, R for the recursive self-improvement loop. Built in the open for developers; early access by waitlist.
